# Design System Specification: Premium Hospitality Operations

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
**Creative North Star: "The Digital Maître d’"**
This design system moves away from the sterile, "software-as-a-service" aesthetic typical of operational tools. Instead, it adopts the persona of a high-end concierge: authoritative, understated, and impeccably organized. 

The system breaks the "template" look by prioritizing **Editorial Intent**. We avoid rigid, boxy grids in favor of intentional asymmetry—using whitespace as a functional element rather than a void. By overlapping high-character serif typography with layered, tonal surfaces, we create a UI that feels curated and bespoke, reflecting the premium nature of the hospitality environments it manages.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
We reject the "default" web. Every color in this palette is chosen to evoke physical materials: heavy ink, aged parchment, and brushed brass.
